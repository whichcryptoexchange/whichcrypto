Slovakia is a small market with a growing group of locally authorised crypto firms, including Tatra banka. NBS publishes unusually practical material for applicants, including a list of the most common gaps it finds in applications.

## The rules

NBS states that from 30 December 2024, "nariadenie MiCA umožňuje poskytovať služby kryptoaktív len na základe povolenia Národnej banky Slovenska" (MiCA allows crypto-asset services to be provided only on the basis of an NBS authorisation).[^nbs-licence] Firms authorised elsewhere in the EU can serve Slovakia through MiCA passporting.

Slovakia chose a 12-month transition rather than MiCA's maximum of 18.[^grandfathering] NBS says that "until December 30, 2025, crypto-asset businesses that are not supervised by the NBS may also operate on our market."[^nbs-crypto] That window has closed.

## Who can apply

NBS specifies the legal forms it accepts: "akciová spoločnosť, spoločnosť s ručením obmedzeným, jednoduchá spoločnosť na akcie, európska spoločnosť (Societas Europea)" (joint-stock company, limited liability company, simple joint-stock company, European company). It adds that "verejná obchodná spoločnosť a fyzická osoba – podnikateľ nie sú vhodnými právnymi formami" (a general partnership and a sole trader are not suitable legal forms).[^nbs-prep]

All MiCA's common requirements also apply: EU seat and management, an EU-resident director, the Article 62 file and €50,000 to €150,000 minimum capital. See [MiCA authorisation explained](/learn/mica-casp-authorisation-explained/).

## Preparing the application

NBS publishes the documents you need:[^nbs-forms]

- the application form
- a checklist of attachments
- a questionnaire on DORA (digital operational resilience) requirements
- a guide to its licence conditions for crypto-asset service providers

On DORA, NBS is explicit that "splnenie týchto požiadaviek musí byť rovnako preukázané už v licenčnom konaní" (meeting these requirements must be demonstrated already during the licensing procedure).[^nbs-forms] It also publishes a page of the most common deficiencies it has found when talking to prospective applicants.[^nbs-forms] Read it before you file.

NBS charges fees for its procedures. Check its [fees page](https://nbs.sk/dohlad-nad-financnym-trhom/poplatky-a-prispevky/poplatky/) for current amounts.

## Pros and cons

**In Slovakia's favour:**
- Practical published material: forms, a checklist, and a list of common deficiencies.[^nbs-forms]

**Things to weigh:**
- Applicants must show DORA compliance during licensing, not after.[^nbs-forms]
- NBS's application guidance pages are in Slovak.
