The UK publishes how often its crypto gateway says yes, and the answer is rarely. It is also in the middle of moving from an anti-money-laundering registration to full authorisation. For the step-by-step process, see our [UK licensing guide](/licensing/gb/). This article covers whether the UK is the right choice.

## What the UK registration is, and isn't

Crypto exchanges and custodian wallet providers doing business in the UK must register with the FCA under the Money Laundering Regulations 2017.[^fincrime] Registration means the FCA supervises the firm for financial crime. It does not mean the FCA has approved its products or conduct. The fee is £11,260 and is not refundable.[^fee-cat][^fees]

## The odds

The FCA publishes its outcomes. Since January 2020 it has received 428 applications and determined 395. Of those, 68 were registered (17%), 266 withdrawn (67%), 47 rejected (12%) and 14 refused (4%), as at 1 November 2026.[^who]

The FCA lists common reasons firms withdraw: "Not being able to show that you meet the required standards at the point of submission", needing time to gather missing information, and "understanding that registration is likely to be refused."[^how] It also says the application "must be complete when you submit it" and that it does not review draft documents.[^how]

## The regime is changing

Applications for authorisation under the Financial Services and Markets Act opened on 30 September 2026, and the new regime is expected to come into force on 25 October 2027.[^regime] Firms already registered will also need authorisation, and "being registered under the MLRs does not guarantee authorisation under FSMA."[^mlr-fsma]

The FCA encourages new applicants to go straight for FSMA authorisation rather than registration, and says firms should not apply for registration after 31 July 2027.[^mlr-fsma] If you are starting now, you are in practice preparing for the new regime.

## Pros and cons

**In the UK's favour:**
- A demanding, transparent gateway: about one in six determined applications ends in registration, and the FCA publishes the figures.[^who]
- Detailed public guidance on what the FCA expects in an application.[^application]

**Things to weigh:**
- No passport. A UK registration covers the UK only.
- It is an AML registration, not a licence, until the FSMA regime starts.[^regime]
- You would face two processes in quick succession: registration now, authorisation by 2027.[^mlr-fsma]
