Many retail trading brands appear on ESMA's MiCA register with Cyprus as their home state. This guide covers what CySEC publishes for applicants and who already holds a Cypriot authorisation.

*Note: CySEC's fee amounts and processing guidance are in PDF documents we haven't reviewed for this article, so they aren't quoted here. MiCA's common requirements apply in full.*

## The authority and the rules

Cyprus's Minister of Finance designated CySEC as the competent authority for MiCA, notified on 7 November 2024.[^cysec-mica] The requirements are MiCA's: an EU registered office, effective management in the EU, at least one EU-resident director, the Article 62 application file, €50,000 to €150,000 minimum capital, and the regulator's 25-plus-40 working day timetable. See [MiCA authorisation explained](/learn/mica-casp-authorisation-explained/).

Cyprus kept MiCA's full 18-month transitional period, which ended on 1 July 2026 at the latest.[^grandfathering]

## CySEC's forms and guidance

CySEC's MiCA page lists the documents applicants and existing firms use, including an application for authorisation as a crypto-asset service provider under Article 62, cross-border notification forms, and questionnaires for board members.[^cysec-mica] It also lists a CySEC directive on MiCA fees and subscriptions (Οδηγία ΟΔ 73-2009-04, December 2024) and the EU technical standard setting out what an application must contain (Regulation (EU) 2025/305).[^cysec-mica]

CySEC's public register separates firms authorised under Article 63 from existing financial firms that notified under Article 60.[^cysec-register] That's useful for counterparties checking which kind of permission a Cypriot firm holds.

## Pros and cons

**In Cyprus's favour:**
- One of the largest groups of home-state entries on ESMA's register, so there is a track record to compare against.
- Published, numbered forms for each stage, and a register that separates authorisations from notifications.[^cysec-mica][^cysec-register]

**Things to weigh:**
- Much of CySEC's MiCA material is published in Greek.[^cysec-mica]
- Check CySEC's fees directive for current costs before budgeting.[^cysec-mica]
